# Brief: Kettle Studio class booking

Kettle Studio is an invented chain of three fitness studios in Manchester. The owner writes:

Members should book classes from an iOS and Android app. They sign in, see the weekly timetable for each studio, book a place, join a waitlist when a class is full, and cancel up to four hours before the class starts. Members pay with a monthly membership or buy class packs in the app. We use Stripe today for our front-desk card machine.

Members should get a push notification when a waitlist place opens and a reminder two hours before a class. Instructors and front-desk staff need a web admin panel to manage the timetable, check members in, and see who booked. Managers want weekly reports on attendance.

The app needs a backend that the app and the admin panel both use. We have two mobile developers who work with an AI coding agent, and one backend developer.

We have no designs yet. We do not have an Apple Developer account. Launch should be before the new year if possible, but nothing is fixed.
