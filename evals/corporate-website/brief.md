# Brief: Alder & Finch Architects website

Alder & Finch is an invented twelve-person architecture practice in Lisbon. The owner writes:

We need a new website to replace our old WordPress site, which is slow and hard to update. The site should show our projects with large photos, explain our services, introduce the team, and let potential clients send an enquiry. Enquiries should land in our HubSpot CRM. We want it in Portuguese and English. Our office manager, not a developer, will add new projects and news posts every month.

We care about looking good on phones, because most visitors come from Instagram. We want to appear in search results for "architecture studio Lisbon" and similar searches. We would like to see how many people visit and which projects they look at.

We have a logo and brand colours but no brand guidelines document. Photos of past projects exist in a shared Google Drive folder. The old site has about 60 pages, and some of them get traffic from Google, so we should not lose those links.

Budget and launch date are not decided yet.
